# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and
professional environment for everyone who participates in this project.

Contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and community members are expected to
communicate respectfully and engage with others in good faith.

## Expected Behaviour

Examples of positive behaviour include:

- Using respectful and inclusive language.
- Accepting constructive feedback professionally.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, methods, or results rather than
  individuals.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.
- Respecting different experiences, backgrounds, and perspectives.
- Supporting collaboration and open technical discussion.
- Acknowledging mistakes and correcting them constructively.
- Disagreeing with research or engineering decisions without making personal
  attacks.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, degrading, threatening, or intimidating comments.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions or collaboration.
- Publishing another person's private or identifying information without
  permission.
- Repeatedly ignoring reasonable project or community guidelines.
- Retaliation against someone who reports inappropriate behaviour in good
  faith.
- Any conduct that creates an unsafe, hostile, or deliberately exclusionary
  environment.

## Technical and Research Disagreement

Disagreement about code, methodology, experimental design, statistical
analysis, interpretation, or documentation is expected in research software.

Constructive criticism is welcome.

Participants should:

- explain technical concerns clearly,
- provide evidence or reproducible examples when possible,
- distinguish criticism of an idea from criticism of a person,
- remain open to correction,
- avoid presenting disagreement as misconduct unless inappropriate behaviour
  has actually occurred.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer or maintainers are responsible for interpreting and
enforcing this Code of Conduct within project spaces.

Possible responses to unacceptable behaviour may include:

- a private or public warning,
- removal or moderation of content,
- temporary participation restrictions,
- rejection or closure of contributions,
- permanent exclusion from project community spaces.

Actions should be proportionate to the nature, severity, and persistence of the
behaviour.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the project maintainer privately
using the contact information available through the maintainer's GitHub
profile.

Please include relevant context and evidence when reasonably possible.

Do not include unrelated credentials, private datasets, sensitive personal
information, or other confidential material in a report.

Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible. Information may
need to be reviewed when necessary to understand or address the reported
conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project-related spaces and interactions,
including:

- Issues.
- Pull requests.
- Discussions.
- Code reviews.
- Project documentation.
- Repository-related communication.
- Other public or private interactions conducted in connection with the
  project.

## Good-Faith Participation

This policy is intended to support respectful collaboration rather than prevent
technical disagreement or legitimate criticism.

Good-faith reports and good-faith technical criticism should not result in
retaliation.
